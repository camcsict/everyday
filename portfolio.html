🚀 Inside Our Computing Quest 💻

From mastering first mouse clicks to engineering advanced Python flight algorithms and responsive web architecture, our students across Grades 1 to 10 are leveling up with hands-on, creative tech missions!

Level 01 Grade 1

🌱 Little Farmers & Mouse Mastery

Our youngest learners stepped into the digital realm as "tech-farmers" on a virtual flower farm! To plant and harvest their blooms, they practiced essential motor coordination: mastering left-clicks, right-clicks, double-clicks, and mouse scrolling. 🌸🖱️

Level 02 Grade 2

🛵 High-Speed "Food Delivery" Quests

Inspired by the Foodpanda delivery riders across Phnom Penh, Grade 2 tackled a high-speed logistics sim! Students received orders, packed goods, and made timely deliveries using click-and-drag mechanics. Speed and customer satisfaction determined their 5-star rating! ⭐📦

Level 03 Grade 3

🐦 Angry Birds & Algorithm Optimization

Team Red on deck! Grade 3 coders guided Red through hazard paths to defend the neighborhood from the green pigs while dodging explosive TNT crates. By deploying loop and repeat blocks, they optimized their algorithm step counts! 💥🧩

❄️ Geometric Art & "Frozen" Snowflakes

💡 Micro:bit Magic: Phnom Penh LED Billboards

Ever wondered what powers the neon LED billboards along Phnom Penh streets? Grade 6 explored embedded hardware with the BBC micro:bit, programming an animated Flashing Heart LED as their debut IoT milestone! ❤️⚡

🧱 Minecraft & Demystifying Data Types

To play and save game states in Minecraft, every player needs an account! Grade 7 investigated user registration systems to master fundamental Data Types:

  • 🔢 Numeric: Phone numbers and player IDs
  • 🔤 String: Custom usernames and secure passwords
  • 🔘 Boolean: Parental consent (True / False)
